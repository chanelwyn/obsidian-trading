## Execution
### Trigger
**Long**
- Break > Opening Range H
- Break > PM H
- Tape Conf
**Short**
- Break < Opening Range L
- Break < PM L
- Tape Conf
### Entry
- Enter only after confirmation.
- Prioritise clean expansion over anticipation.
- Avoid chasing extended candles.
### Stop
Use nearest structural invalidation.
Examples
- Opposite Opening Range
- PM H / PM L
- Initial pullback low/high
- Intraday swing
### Targets
Primary
- Daily S/R
- Measured move
- Trend continuation
- Gap objective
Management target
- Trail using prior 2m bar once trend is established.
### Management
- Hold while momentum remains intact.
- Respect the 2m trailing stop.
- Scale according to plan.
- Do not exit solely because of unrealised P&L.
### Invalidation
Exit immediately if:
- Opening Drive fails
- Price loses Opening Range
- Tape reverses aggressively
- Market direction changes materially
- Thesis becomes invalid

## Review
### Common Mistakes
- Trading without an A+ daily chart.
- Chasing extended moves.
- Entering before confirmation.
- Ignoring market regime.
- Holding after Opening Drive failure.
- Trading against sector strength.

### Coach Notes
> [!important]
> Opening Drive is a context-dependent setup. Daily quality determines whether aggressive execution is justified.
- A gap below major daily support supports Opening Drive Shorts.
- A gap above major resistance supports Opening Drive Longs.
- If the opening move becomes unclear, wait for another qualified setup instead of forcing an Opening Drive.
- In strong bull markets, prioritise long Opening Drives over low-quality shorts.
