# Multi-Day Continuation
## Purpose
Trade continuation after a high-conviction Day 1 move. Institutions establish a position on Day 1, pause with orderly consolidation, then continue the trend on Day 2 or Day 3.
This is one of the highest-priority long setups during strong bull market regimes.

---
## Qualification
### Market Context
- Strong bull regime preferred
- Major catalyst or theme
- Strong Day 1 momentum
- High institutional participation
- Sector leadership
- SPY / QQQ supportive
### Required Conditions
#### Day 1
- Large directional move
- High RVOL
- Close near HOD
- Strong catalyst
#### Day 2 / Day 3
- Tight consolidation
- ↓Vol
- Holds above key support
- No significant profit-taking
- Buyers remain in control
- Room to next D resistance
### Checklist
- [ ] Strong Day 1 trend
- [ ] Catalyst confirmed
- [ ] RVOL elevated
- [ ] Tight consolidation
- [ ] ↓Vol
- [ ] Key support holding
- [ ] Trigger defined
- [ ] Risk defined before entry
---
## Execution
### Trigger
- Break > consolidation H
- Break > prior D H
- Tape Conf
- Buyers lifting offers
### Entry
- Enter on confirmed breakout.
- Avoid chasing extended candles.
- Add only after successful continuation.
### Stop
Use nearest structural invalidation.
Examples
- Below consolidation L
- Below Day 2 L
- Below breakout level
- Below key support
### Targets
Primary
- Measured move
- Next D resistance
- Trend continuation
- New HOD / ATH
Management target
- Trail using prior 2m bar.
### Management
- Expect orderly continuation.
- Respect the 2m trailing stop.
- Allow the trend to develop.
- Scale according to plan.
### Invalidation
Exit immediately if:
- Consolidation fails
- Breakout is rejected
- High-volume distribution appears
- Market regime changes materially
- Catalyst loses relevance
---
## Review
### Common Mistakes
- Trading without a strong Day 1.
- Buying loose consolidations.
- Ignoring declining relative strength.
- Chasing after breakout extension.
- Selling because of unrealised P&L.
- Treating every gap as Day 2 continuation.

### Coach Notes
> [!important]
> Strong Day 1 + tight Day 2 = institutional continuation. Loose consolidation = lower probability.
- Prioritise during strong bull markets.
- ↓Vol during consolidation is a quality characteristic.
- Day 3 setups should remain near the Day 1 extreme, not deep inside the prior range.
- A [[Breakout Long]] often becomes the execution trigger inside this setup.
