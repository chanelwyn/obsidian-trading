# Capitulation Reversal Long
## Purpose
Buy an exhaustion reversal after panic selling has largely completed. The objective is to capture the first high-probability mean reversion rather than catch the falling knife.

---
## Qualification
### Market Context
- Daily chart oversold or extended
- Selling climax approaching exhaustion
- High relative volume (≥2x)
- Major support nearby
- No fresh bearish catalyst invalidating reversal
- Market regime supports mean reversion
### Required Conditions
- Capitulation volume present (**mandatory**)
- Price far from EMA/VWAP
- Selling momentum slowing
- First higher low or right-side reversal forming
- Buyers begin absorbing supply
- Room to EMA/VWAP or next resistance
### Checklist
- [ ] Daily oversold
- [ ] ≥2x RVOL
- [ ] Support identified
- [ ] Capitulation confirmed
- [ ] Right-side reversal forming
- [ ] Trigger level defined
- [ ] Risk defined before entry
---
## Execution
### Trigger
- Break > first reversal candle H
- Higher low confirmed
- Tape Conf
- Buyers lifting offers
### Entry
- Enter only after confirmation.
- Never average into a falling knife.
- Add only after momentum confirms.
### Stop
Use nearest structural invalidation.
Examples:
- Below capitulation low
- Below reversal swing
- Below support
### Targets
Primary
- VWAP
- EMA
- Gap fill
- Intraday resistance
Management target
- Scale into strength.
- Trail remaining position using prior 2m bar.
### Management
- Immediate momentum is expected.
- Reduce exposure if momentum disappears.
- Scale according to plan.
- Respect predefined risk.
### Invalidation
Exit immediately if:
- Capitulation low fails
- Reversal stalls after entry
- Sellers regain control
- Fresh bearish news appears
- Tape rejects higher prices
---
## Review
### Common Mistakes
- Catching the falling knife.
- No capitulation volume.
- Entering before confirmation.
- Averaging down.
- Holding a stalled reversal.
- Confusing a dead-cat bounce with a true reversal.

### Coach Notes
> [!warning]
> No ≥2x volume spike → No Capitulation Reversal Long.
- Wait for the **right side of the V**.
- This is a mean-reversion setup, not a trend-following trade.
- If the bounce stalls immediately after entry, exit quickly.
- Context overrides pattern; major news may invalidate or strengthen the setup.
