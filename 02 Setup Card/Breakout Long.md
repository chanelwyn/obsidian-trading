# Breakout Long
## Purpose
Buy institutional continuation as price breaks through established resistance after orderly accumulation.
Unlike the [[Opening Drive]], this setup focuses on **holding strength** before expansion rather than the initial opening impulse.

---
## Qualification
### Market Context
- Bullish daily bias
- Strong catalyst or sector theme
- High RVOL
- Strong relative strength
- SPY / QQQ supportive
- Adequate liquidity
### Required Conditions
- Strong initial advance
- Tight consolidation near HOD
- ↓Vol during consolidation
- Buyers holding the ask
- Resistance tested multiple times
- Clear upside room
### Checklist
- [ ] Daily trend bullish
- [ ] Catalyst confirmed
- [ ] RVOL elevated
- [ ] Tight consolidation
- [ ] ↓Vol
- [ ] Ask holding
- [ ] Trigger defined
- [ ] Risk defined before entry
---
## Execution
### Trigger
- Break > consolidation H
- Break > OR H / PM H
- Tape Conf
- Ask lifted with expanding volume
### Entry
- Enter on confirmed breakout.
- Prefer first clean expansion.
- Avoid chasing extended candles.
### Stop
Use nearest structural invalidation.
Examples
- Below consolidation L
- Below breakout level
- Below VWAP
- Below swing L
### Targets
Primary
- Measured move
- Next D resistance
- HOD extension
- Trend continuation
Management target
- Trail using prior 2m bar.
### Management
- Expect expansion immediately after breakout.
- Hold while higher lows remain intact.
- Scale according to plan.
- Respect the 2m trailing stop.
### Invalidation
Exit immediately if:
- Breakout fails
- Price loses consolidation
- Heavy distribution appears
- Tape weakens materially
- Market thesis changes
---
## Review
### Common Mistakes
- Buying extended breakouts.
- Trading without consolidation.
- Ignoring declining volume quality.
- Buying into nearby resistance.
- Selling because of unrealised P&L.
- Entering before buyers commit.
