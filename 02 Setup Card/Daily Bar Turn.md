# Daily Bar Turn
## Purpose
Short the transition from front-side strength to backside weakness after the higher-timeframe trend has objectively reversed.
This setup captures the **first high-probability continuation** after the uptrend has broken. The Backside provides the context; subsequent [[Bouncy Ball Short]] setups become the preferred trigger.

---
## Qualification
### Market Context
- Extended daily uptrend
- Daily weakness developing
- Prior D bar L broken
- Failed opening strength
- Sector weakening
- Bearish market context preferred
### Required Conditions
- Prior D bar L breached (**mandatory**)
- Front-side trend completed
- LH + LL confirmed
- Failed recovery into resistance
- Room to next D support
- No major support immediately below
### Checklist
- [ ] Prior D bar L broken
- [ ] Backside confirmed
- [ ] LH formed
- [ ] LL confirmed
- [ ] Recovery failed
- [ ] Trigger level defined
- [ ] Risk defined before entry
---
## Execution
### Trigger
- Break < intraday support
- Break < PM L or D L
- Tape Conf
- Seller aggression increasing
### Entry
- Enter after the backside is confirmed.
- Never short the front-side squeeze.
- Add only after continuation confirms.
### Stop
Use nearest structural invalidation.
Examples
- Above LH
- Above failed bounce
- Above VWAP
- Above intraday resistance
### Targets
Primary
- D support
- PM L
- Measured move
- Trend continuation
Management target
- Trail using prior 2m bar.
### Management
- Allow trend continuation to develop.
- Hold while LH/LL structure remains intact.
- Scale only into planned levels.
- Respect the 2m trailing stop.
### Invalidation
Exit immediately if:
- LH fails
- Price reclaims resistance
- Buyers absorb the breakdown
- Daily thesis becomes invalid
- Market regime shifts materially
---
## Review
### Common Mistakes
- Shorting the front side.
- Assuming a large red candle equals a backside.
- Entering before LH confirmation.
- Chasing the breakdown.
- Exiting because of unrealised P&L.
- Ignoring higher-timeframe support.

### Coach Notes
> [!warning]
> Prior D bar L not breached → Not a Daily Bar Turn.
- Wait for the trend to objectively change.
- The first valid short occurs **after** LH + LL confirmation.
- Once the backside is established, look for [[Bouncy Ball Short]] entries on subsequent pullbacks.
- This setup prevents repeatedly shorting the strongest part of an uptrend.
