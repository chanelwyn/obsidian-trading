# Daily Operating System
> [!summary]
> Objective: Arrive at the opening bell with a clear bias, defined risk, and only A-grade opportunities.
## Workflow
```text
Morning
    ↓
Market Regime
    ↓
Watchlist
    ↓
Trade Plan
    ↓
Go / No-Go
```
---
## 1. Morning Reset
> [!check]- Readiness
- [ ] Sleep, health and focus acceptable
- [ ] Trading platform working
- [ ] Internet stable
- [ ] News feeds online
- [ ] No emotional baggage
- [ ] Kill Switch reviewed
---
## 2. Market Regime
> [!check]- Environment First
- [ ] SPY / QQQ bias
- [ ] Market regime identified
- [ ] Volatility acceptable
- [ ] Sector leaders identified
- [ ] Major economic events checked
> [!note]
> Regime determines setup selection. Never force a setup against the regime.
See:
- [[Market Regimes]]
- [[Combined Setup File]]
---
## 3. Watchlist
> [!check]- Candidate Selection
For each ticker:
- [ ] Catalyst confirmed
- [ ] Daily bias confirmed
- [ ] Setup identified
- [ ] Liquidity acceptable
- [ ] Relative volume sufficient
- [ ] Key levels marked
Reject immediately if:
- No catalyst
- No edge
- Poor liquidity
- No room to target
---
## 4. Trade Planning
> [!check]- Before Market Open
For every planned trade:
- [ ] Setup grade assigned
- [ ] Entry trigger defined
- [ ] Stop defined
- [ ] Target defined
- [ ] Position size calculated
- [ ] Maximum risk accepted
See:
- [[Trade Execution]]
---
## 5. Go / No-Go
### GO
- A-grade market
- A-grade setup
- Risk defined
- Full concentration
### NO-GO
- Low-quality market
- No valid setup
- Emotional state compromised
- Platform issues
- Risk cannot be defined
---
## Operating Rules
> [!important]
>
> - Context before setup.
> - Setup before entry.
> - Entry before size.
> - Size before execution.
> - No trade is a valid decision.
---
## Decision Hierarchy
```text
Safety
    ↓
Market Regime
    ↓
Daily Bias
    ↓
Setup
    ↓
Trigger
    ↓
Risk
    ↓
Execution
```
See:
- [[Risk & Discipline]]
- [[Trade Execution]]
---
## End State
Before the opening bell:
- [ ] Bias is clear
- [ ] Watchlist finalised
- [ ] Levels marked
- [ ] Risk defined
- [ ] No unresolved decisions
> [!success]
> Trade only when preparation is complete. Waiting is part of execution.
