# Review & Improvement
> [!summary]
> Objective: Review the process, not the outcome. Every trading day ends with deliberate improvement.
---
## Review Workflow
```text
Close Market
      ↓
Journal
      ↓
Review Trades
      ↓
Identify Lessons
      ↓
Create Actions
      ↓
Prepare Tomorrow
```
---
## 1. End-of-Day Shutdown
> [!check]- Before Leaving the Desk
- [ ] All positions closed (unless planned swing)
- [ ] Orders cancelled
- [ ] Platform backed up
- [ ] Charts saved
- [ ] Screenshots organised
---
## 2. Trade Journal
> [!check]- Every Trade
- [ ] Thesis recorded
- [ ] Setup identified
- [ ] Entry documented
- [ ] Exit documented
- [ ] Screenshots attached
- [ ] Emotions noted
Reference:
- [[Trade Review Intake]]
---
## 3. Process Review
> [!check]- Evaluate Execution
For every trade ask:
- [ ] Was the setup valid?
- [ ] Was the entry rule-based?
- [ ] Was risk respected?
- [ ] Was management disciplined?
- [ ] Was the exit according to plan?
> [!note]
> Judge the process before the P&L.
Reference:
- [[Trading Coach]]
- [[Trade Scoring Rubric]]
---
## 4. Rule Violations
> [!check]- Identify Errors
Document every violation.
Examples:
- [ ] Early entry
- [ ] Chasing
- [ ] Oversized position
- [ ] Ignored stop
- [ ] Emotional exit
- [ ] Unplanned trade
- [ ] Broke daily loss rules
---
## 5. Lessons Learned
> [!check]- Continuous Improvement
Record:
- [ ] One thing to repeat
- [ ] One mistake to eliminate
- [ ] One rule to reinforce
- [ ] One setup to study
Avoid vague conclusions.
---
## 6. Tomorrow's Preparation
> [!check]- Reset
- [ ] Review watchlist
- [ ] Review market regime
- [ ] Study one benchmark trade
- [ ] Clear mental state
> [!important]
>
> Tomorrow starts tonight.
---
## Weekly Reflection
Complete at the end of each trading week.
- [ ] Win rate
- [ ] Average R
- [ ] Best setup
- [ ] Worst mistake
- [ ] Biggest improvement
- [ ] One objective for next week
---
## Monthly Reflection
- [ ] Performance reviewed
- [ ] Statistics updated
- [ ] Playbook refined
- [ ] Rules adjusted (if evidence supports)
> [!warning]
>
> Improve the system only after sufficient data. Never rewrite rules because of one trade.
---
## Daily Success Criteria
A successful day means:
- [ ] Rules followed
- [ ] Risk protected
- [ ] Journal completed
- [ ] Lessons captured
- [ ] Ready for tomorrow
> [!success]
> Consistency compounds. Great traders improve their process every day.
