# Improvement Tracker

> [!important]
> Track recurring weaknesses until they become consistent strengths. Record patterns, not isolated mistakes.

## Purpose

Maintain a single log of recurring improvements to:

- Eliminate repeated mistakes
- Build better trading habits
- Reinforce correct behaviours
- Measure progress over time
- Prioritise coaching focus

## Recurring Mistakes

Record only mistakes that occur multiple times.

Examples:

- Entering before confirmation
- Moving stop loss
- Oversizing positions
- Ignoring market context
- Taking non-qualified setups

Do not include one-off errors.

---

## Habit Development

Track habits worth reinforcing.

Examples:

- Waiting for confirmation
- Respecting stop loss
- Completing trade reviews
- Following daily routine
- Trading only documented setups

---

## Resolution Criteria

An improvement may be marked **Resolved** when:

- The issue has not recurred over a meaningful sample of trades.
- The correct behaviour has become consistent.
- Recent trade reviews confirm sustained improvement.

If the issue returns, reopen it instead of creating a duplicate entry.

---

## Review Schedule

Review this tracker during:

- Weekly review
- Monthly review
- Quarterly review (optional)

Archive resolved items only if they remain consistently resolved.

---

## Do Not

- Record every individual mistake.
- Duplicate information from trade reviews.
- Track P&L goals.
- Modify permanent trading rules.
