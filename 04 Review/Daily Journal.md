# Daily Journal

> [!important]
> Record observations from today's trading. Journals capture experience—they do not change permanent trading rules.

## Purpose

Document each trading day to:

- Capture market observations
- Record execution quality
- Monitor discipline
- Reflect on psychology
- Prepare for tomorrow

## Psychology

Record observations such as:

- Discipline
- Patience
- Confidence
- Emotional control
- Decision quality

Describe behaviours, not emotions alone.

---

## Lessons Learned

Record only lessons from today's trading.

Good examples:

- Wait for confirmation before sizing.
- Avoid forcing trades during slow sessions.
- Follow the planned exit.

Avoid rewriting permanent trading rules.

---

## Tomorrow's Focus

Choose 1–3 actionable priorities.

Example:

- Improve entry patience.
- Reduce unnecessary trades.
- Follow stop-loss without hesitation.
