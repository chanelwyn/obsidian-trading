# Periodic Review

> [!important]
> Review long-term performance to identify trends and improve the trading process. Focus on repeated behaviours rather than individual trades.

## Purpose

Conduct structured reviews on a recurring schedule to:

- Measure process consistency
- Identify recurring strengths and weaknesses
- Review trading performance
- Prioritise improvements
- Define objectives for the next review period

---

## Review Frequency

| Review | Frequency |
|---------|-----------|
| Weekly | Every trading week |
| Monthly | End of each month |
| Quarterly | Optional |

## Process Review

Evaluate:

- Setup selection
- Execution quality
- Risk management
- Trade management
- Discipline
- Review consistency

---

## Recurring Strengths

Document behaviours to continue.

Example:

- Waited for confirmation.
- Respected daily loss limits.
- Executed only A-grade setups.

---

## Recurring Weaknesses

Document repeated problems.

Example:

- Chased late entries.
- Reduced patience after losses.
- Overtraded slow markets.

Recurring issues should be tracked in [[Improvement Tracker]].

---

---

## Rule Review

Record:

- Frequently followed rules
- Frequently violated rules
- Rules requiring additional practice

Reference the [[Master Trading System Rulebook]] instead of duplicating rules.

---

## Improvement Priorities

Limit to 1–3 priorities.

Example:

1. Improve trade selection.
2. Reduce execution hesitation.
3. Increase review consistency.

---

## Next Review Goals

Define measurable objectives.

Examples:

- No FOMO trades.
- 100% review completion.
- Execute only documented setups.
- Improve rule compliance.

---

## Follow-Up

Update when required:

- [[Improvement Tracker]]
- [[Progress Tracker]]
- [[Performance Dashboard]]

---

## Do Not

- Change permanent trading rules.
- Focus only on P&L.
- Create too many objectives.
- Ignore recurring behavioural patterns.
